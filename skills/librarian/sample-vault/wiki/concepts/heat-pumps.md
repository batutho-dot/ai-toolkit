# Heat pumps

A heat pump moves heat from a cold source to a warmer sink using work. The ratio of heat
delivered to work put in is the coefficient of performance. Seasonal performance depends on
the source temperature, the sink temperature and the control strategy over the year, so a
single rated figure says little about a whole heating season. See [[district-heating]] for
large units that feed a network, and the example source (Example and Author, 2020, p. 4).
