# District heating
A network of pipes that carries hot water from central plants to buildings. Large heat pumps,
waste heat and boilers feed it. Losses grow with pipe length and supply temperature, which is
why newer networks run cooler. Back to [[heat-pumps]]. Source: https://example.org/district-heating
